FOR DEVELOPERS

Built to Integrate, Built to Hold

The complaints we hear most are not about models — they are about everything around them. So the plumbing is part of the product.

One Integration, OpenAI-Compatible

Point your existing OpenAI client at the exchange endpoint and switch models by name. No new SDK to learn, no chain of aggregators stacking errors and margin between you and the model.

Limits in the Open

The caps on your account are the ones you set on your own tokens — visible in the console, never tightened silently mid-month. Every call lands on a per-token statement you can export and reconcile.

Failover by Default

Failed requests retry automatically across redundant channels, and a failing channel is pulled out of rotation instead of cascading errors into your product. One supplier's bad day is not your outage.
